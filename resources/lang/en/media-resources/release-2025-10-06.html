Cuenca’s Animal Welfare Organizations Unite to Form Historic Coalition

Cuenca, Ecuador — Thursday, September 25th — In a landmark step for animal welfare in Cuenca, three of Cuenca, Ecuador’s animal welfare organizations—FAAN (Fundación Familia Amor Animal), PATÁN Animal Rescue, and Segundas Oportunidades-Refugio Temporal—have officially signed an agreement to form The Animal Welfare Coalition of Cuenca, Ecuador.

This unprecedented partnership reflects a shared vision: a Cuenca where animals live free from abuse, abandonment, and neglect, and where compassion for animals is seen as integral to the health and values of the community.

“By joining together, we are stronger,” said coalition representatives. “This coalition demonstrates that when organizations collaborate rather than compete, we can achieve greater impact, inspire a cultural movement of care, and give animals the second chances they deserve.”

A United Front for Change

The coalition will work collaboratively in six key areas:

  • Advocacy & Government Engagement: Presenting a unified voice in dialogue with authorities to strengthen animal protection laws, policies, and enforcement.
  • Public Education & Awareness: Launching community campaigns on responsible guardianship, adoption, and spay/neuter to reduce abandonment.
  • Resource Development: Leveraging joint fundraising and grant opportunities to expand impact.
  • Adoption & Rescue Support: Creating a shared adoption platform and supporting emergency rescues together.
  • Creation of a Trusted Coalition Affiliated Veterinarian Network
  • Knowledge Sharing: Exchanging best practices and training to strengthen each organization’s capacity.

First Priority: Inspiring a Culture of Care

One of the coalition’s first initiatives will be the launch of a public awareness campaign to inspire a city-wide movement of care and responsibility for animals. The campaign will also invite government partners, sponsors, and community members to join the coalition in building a safer, more compassionate Cuenca for animals.

A Call to Action

The Coalition for Animal Welfare in Cuenca is calling on citizens, businesses, educators, and public officials to stand with them. Together, Cuenca can become a model city where the dignity and wellbeing of animals are recognized as essential to a thriving and compassionate society.
